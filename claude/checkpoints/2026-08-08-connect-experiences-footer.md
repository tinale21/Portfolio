# Checkpoint — Let's Connect section, Experiences section, site-wide Footer

## Context

Three new pieces completing the homepage scroll sequence and the site's global chrome: a scroll-driven "Let's Connect" photo-reveal section (after Philosophy), a static "Experiences" list section, and a Footer added to the root layout so it appears on every route, not just the homepage.

## Human directions

- "Let's Connect": gave a prose animation spec up front (dark bg constant throughout, photos not immediately visible, staggered non-simultaneous entrance, reference video for *style only* — "It should not change the final layout"), a motion reference video and a live reference site to analyze before sending real assets, then sent 6 photos + 7 Figma dev-mode inspect screenshots once satisfied with the motion analysis, explicitly noting "i don't have it exact for position since its some images out of frame that scrolls in."
- After first build, corrected twice in a row: (1) "the 'Let's Connect' stays in the middle and the photos are suppose to scroll down as users scroll down" — rejected the radial fly-in-to-fixed-position model; (2) "the photos don't continuing moving out of frame as the users scrolls" — rejected the clamped settle-and-freeze model even after direction was fixed.
- "let's build out the next section" (Experiences) — provided 26 Figma dev-mode screenshots covering every row's text content/typography and the divider styling, plus asked to see it flow directly after Connect before revisiting Connect's open items.
- Iterative small polish on Experiences: heading size 36→28→32px, row text 24→19→16px, heading matched to Projects' heading (font/size/weight, then explicitly also font-family and color), heading-to-list spacing tightened, row-list side-padding nudged out then reverted back to flush with the heading.
- "Let's add the footer" — gave 2 asset files (blacklogo.svg, skill-icons_linkedin.svg) and 10 Figma dev-mode screenshots; when asked for the LinkedIn profile URL (not present anywhere in the codebase), provided `www.linkedin.com/in/tina-le-aab63a296` directly rather than having it guessed.
- Iterative footer polish: all text 24→16px, logo/LinkedIn icon scaled down to match (77×65→51×43, 34×34→23×23), vertical padding tightened then walked back up twice ("increase it a little bit" / "increase it a bit more").

## Records of resistance / things I got wrong and had to correct

- **Connect section motion, round 1**: built the heading at Figma's literal (off-center) coordinate and animated each photo along a radial vector from the frame's center — user rejected both. Re-verified against the actual motion reference (extracted video frames + sampled the live reference site's DOM across its full scroll range) instead of re-guessing: found the heading sits in a genuinely `position: sticky` layer with a bit-for-bit constant transform (never moves), and photos have a constant `left` with only `top` changing — vertical motion only, no diagonal. Rebuilt on that evidence: heading dead-center via absolute+translate, photos animate `y` only.
- **Connect section motion, round 2**: after the direction fix, still built photos to clamp and freeze at their Figma rest position once reached. User: "the photos don't continuing moving out of frame." The same live-site DOM sample already contained the answer (each photo's `top` changed continuously across the entire scroll range, never plateauing) but I hadn't drawn that conclusion the first time. Replaced the clamped `useTransform([start,end], ...)` per photo with a 3-point unclamped line (`[0, arrival, 1] → [ENTRY_BEFORE, 0, -ENTRY_AFTER]`) so every photo keeps rising and exits above the frame (clipped by the sticky container's own `overflow-hidden`) instead of stopping.
- **Connect section, photo2's y-position**: Figma's dev-mode "Layer properties" diagram gave two candidate readings (top-distance vs bottom-distance) with no way to disambiguate from the screenshot alone. Rendered both; one placed the photo directly on top of the heading text (unreadable) — used that as the tiebreaker rather than guessing blind. photo3's equivalent ambiguity had no such tiebreaker and is still a best-guess, flagged in code.
- Every Experiences/Footer sizing request this round was the user directly dialing in a value through trial (make it smaller → smaller still → heading specifically 32px → match Projects' weight/size → also match font-family → also make it black), not a correction of a wrong assumption — each was applied literally and verified via `tsc`/dev-server refresh rather than second-guessed.

## Successes

- For Connect's motion, leaned on **direct measurement over re-reading prose**: extracting video frames with ffmpeg and sampling the live site's DOM (`getComputedStyle().transform` across 5 evenly-spaced scroll positions) settled both direction and clamping questions with hard evidence, rather than another round of eyeballing screenshots.
- Cross-checked every Figma-derived photo position two ways (`left + width = frameWidth - right`) before trusting it, exactly matching the rigor already established for Hero/Philosophy — caught that 4 of 6 Connect photos had fully-confirmable X positions, and was honest about the 2 that didn't rather than presenting them as equally certain.
- Footer: reused NavBar's exact route hrefs (`/`, `/work`, `/about`) instead of re-typing them, and asked for the LinkedIn URL rather than fabricating one, per the standing rule against guessing URLs.
- Recognized Footer belongs in `src/app/layout.tsx` (alongside NavBar, which is already there) rather than only in the homepage's `page.tsx` — confirmed it renders on `/work` too before calling it done, instead of assuming.
